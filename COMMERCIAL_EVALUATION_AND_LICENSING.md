Subject to written negotiation, Clarity Structures Digital S.L. may offer:

- controlled technical evaluation;
- proof-of-capability or pilot agreements;
- internal-use commercial licenses;
- deployment and integration agreements;
- source-code access under confidentiality;
- source-code escrow;
- sector-specific licensing;
- strategic partnerships;
- investment in Clarity Structures Digital S.L.;
- exclusive licensing;
- partial or complete acquisition of defined intellectual-property rights.

## Anti-flapping acceptance criteria

A commercial implementation may define measurable criteria including:

- stable replay for repeated equivalent requests;
- conflict rejection for inconsistent key reuse;
- a single authoritative active execution lease;
- stale-worker fencing;
- controlled reacquisition after expiry;
- bounded duplicate effects within the agreed transactional scope;
- evidence of decisions, attempts and terminal outcomes.

These criteria apply only to the expressly defined system boundary.

No agreement should describe the product as universally immune to network
failures or guarantee exactly-once behaviour outside that boundary.

## External services

Telecommunications APIs, cloud platforms, WAF services, event buses and other
third-party systems are governed by their own availability, contracts, terms
and technical limits.

Their integration does not make Clarity Structures Digital S.L. responsible
for guarantees that those third parties have not contractually assumed.

## Investment and technical direction

Investment, board rights, reserved matters, technical leadership, founder
continuity, transfer of intellectual property and approval of exclusive

employment, management or acquisition agreements.

This document is not an offer, commitment or binding commercial agreement.
